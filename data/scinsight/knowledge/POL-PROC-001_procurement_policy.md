# Cosmetics procurement policy
## Approved suppliers
Only suppliers flagged approved in the SAP supplier master may be used for production materials. Unvetted suppliers
(for example Unvetted Pumps Ltd) require a supplier qualification and a quality agreement before any purchase.

## Purchase requests
A purchase request above EUR 10,000 needs the procurement manager's approval. Emergency purchases must document the
production order at risk and the shortage quantity calculated from SAP data.

## Lead times
Planning uses the supplier's demonstrated lead time from goods receipts when at least three receipts exist; otherwise the
contract lead time. Expediting a confirmed purchase order requires written supplier confirmation of the new date.
