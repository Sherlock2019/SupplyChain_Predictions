# Quality SOP — batch release
## Release
A batch may be used or shipped only after the quality manager's usage decision "released" in SAP. Batches in quality
inspection and blocked batches must not be used, shipped or planned.

## Blocked stock
A blocked batch stays blocked until the deviation is closed. The agent may report blocked quantities but never plan them.
