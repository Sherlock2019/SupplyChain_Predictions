# Supply Agreement BA-2026-14 — BioActive Labs GmbH (vendor V-100318)

## Scope

BioActive Labs supplies the serum active complex RM-ACTIVE to the Lyon skincare factory FR20. The agreement runs from 1 January 2026 to 31 December 2027 and covers all FG-SERUM-50 production volume.

## Lead time and service level

The contractual replenishment lead time is 28 calendar days from purchase-order confirmation. The supplier commits to an on-time-in-full (OTIF) service level of 95% measured on a rolling six-month basis. A delivery counts as on time when it is received no later than the confirmed delivery date and in full when at least 98% of the ordered quantity passes incoming inspection.

## Expedite clause

Aurelia may request an expedite of any confirmed order once per quarter. BioActive Labs must respond within two business days and can pull in delivery by up to ten calendar days for a surcharge of 8% of the order value. Expedite requests must be approved by the supply-chain manager before procurement contacts the supplier. No agent may place, change, or expedite a purchase order in SAP.

## Secondary source

There is currently no qualified secondary source for RM-ACTIVE. Qualification of Seoul Actives Co. is in progress (quality project QP-2026-031, expected completion Q2 2027). Until then, single-source risk must be flagged in every supplier review.

## Quality obligations

Every batch must ship with a certificate of analysis (CoA), an allergen statement, and microbiological results. Batches without complete documentation are placed in quality inspection and cannot be used until the quality department records a usage decision in SAP QM.

## Penalties

If OTIF falls below 90% for two consecutive quarters, Aurelia may apply a service credit of 3% of quarterly spend and may trigger a formal supplier improvement plan.
