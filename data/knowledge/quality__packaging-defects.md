# QP-07 — Primary packaging defect classification

## Critical defects

Critical defects include neck-finish dimensions out of tolerance, cracks, and contamination. A critical defect leads to rejection of the whole lot. Neck-finish defects on perfume bottles create pump-crimp leakage risk and must never be released under deviation.

## Major and minor defects

Major defects (scratches in the decoration zone, colour deviation) may be released under a documented deviation approved by the quality manager. Minor defects are logged for supplier scorecards only.
