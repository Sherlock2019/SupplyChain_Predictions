# LP-05 — Carrier service levels

## Contracted carriers

Emirates SkyCargo (air, Paris–Dubai, SLA 3 days, 95% on-time), Maersk (sea, Dubai–Singapore, SLA 16 days, 88% on-time), CMA CGM (sea, Asia–Europe inbound, SLA 42 days), DHL Freight (road, EU inbound, SLA 6 days), Geodis (road, France domestic, SLA 2 days).

## Escalation

A delay above the carrier SLA triggers a claim review. Customs holds in Dubai (DXB) are typically resolved within 48 hours once the commercial invoice and certificate of origin are re-submitted.
