# Supply Agreement AC-2025-07 — AromaChem SAS (vendor V-100231)

## Scope

AromaChem SAS supplies fragrance concentrate RM-FRAGRANCE to the Paris fragrance factory FR10 for the Eau de Parfum 100 ml (FG-PERFUME-100) range.

## Lead time and service level

Contractual lead time is 21 calendar days. Target OTIF is 95%. AromaChem holds a consignment safety stock of 40 kg at its Grasse site which can be called off within 5 days with manager approval.

## Secondary source

Grasse Essences SARL is a qualified secondary source for RM-FRAGRANCE (qualification QP-2025-012 closed). Switching volume to the secondary source requires a formula equivalence check by R&D and manager approval.

## Quality obligations

Each lot requires an IFRA conformity certificate, allergen declaration (EU 2023/1545 labelling), and gas-chromatography fingerprint matching the master standard within tolerance.
