# QP-01 — Cosmetics raw-material quality release policy

## Release principle

Raw-material batches in quality inspection must not be counted as available for production. A batch becomes usable only when the quality department records an "accepted" usage decision in SAP QM.

## Required documentation

High-risk active ingredients (RM-ACTIVE) require a certificate of analysis, an allergen statement, microbiological results, and stability data. Fragrance concentrates require IFRA conformity. Missing documents keep the batch in inspection.

## Current open lots

## Expired material

Expired batches are blocked automatically and must be scrapped or re-tested. Re-testing is allowed once for bulk bases with a maximum shelf-life extension of 90 days, subject to stability evidence.
