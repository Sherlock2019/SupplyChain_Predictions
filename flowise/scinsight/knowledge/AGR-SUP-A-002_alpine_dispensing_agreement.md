# Supply agreement — Alpine Dispensing (pump dispensers)
## Scope
Alpine Dispensing (synthetic) supplies 24/410 pump dispensers (PUMP-DISP-24) and pump overcaps to the Lyon factory.

## Lead time and minimum order
Contract lead time: 5 working days from purchase order confirmation. Minimum order quantity: 5,000 units.
On-time-in-full target: 95 percent per quarter.

## Expediting
Orders may be expedited by up to 2 days for a surcharge of 8 percent of the order value, subject to written confirmation.
