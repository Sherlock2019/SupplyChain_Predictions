# Production SOP — material readiness check
## When to check
Material readiness is checked 10 working days before the production start and again 3 days before release.

## How readiness is calculated
Required quantity = planned quantity x BOM quantity per unit x (1 + scrap). Only released, unexpired, unreserved stock at
the producing plant counts. Inbound supply counts only when the purchase order is confirmed and promised before the start.

## When a component is short
The planner proposes: expedite or reorder from an approved supplier, produce a partial quantity, or move the production
order. The production planner decides; nothing is changed in SAP without that decision.
