# Material specification — 24/410 pump dispenser
## Specification
PUMP-DISP-24: 24/410 neck finish, 0.20 ml dose, polypropylene, compatible with BTL-GLS-30 amber glass bottles.
One pump dispenser is required per 30 ml serum bottle (BOM quantity 1 EA).

## Substitutions
Pumps from a different supplier are interchangeable only after a compatibility test with the serum formula and a
quality release of the first batch.
